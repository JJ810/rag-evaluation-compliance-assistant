# Data Retention Policy

## Purpose

This synthetic policy defines retention and deletion expectations for example enterprise data classes.

## Requirements

Customer support tickets are retained for three years after ticket closure unless a legal hold requires longer retention.

Security event logs are retained for one year. Critical incident evidence is retained for seven years after the incident is closed.

User deletion requests must be reviewed and completed within 30 calendar days when no legal hold or regulatory exception applies.

Encrypted operational backups are retained for 35 days. Backup restoration tests must be performed at least quarterly.

## Evidence

Acceptable evidence includes retention schedules, deletion request tickets, backup configuration exports, and legal hold approvals.
