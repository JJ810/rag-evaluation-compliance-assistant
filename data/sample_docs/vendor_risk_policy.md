# Vendor Risk Management Policy

## Purpose

This synthetic policy defines review expectations for third-party vendors and SaaS providers.

## Requirements

New vendors that process customer data must complete a security review before contract signature or production data access.

High-risk vendors must provide current security documentation such as SOC 2 reports, penetration test summaries, data flow diagrams, and incident notification procedures.

Vendor risk reviews must be refreshed annually for high-risk vendors and every two years for moderate-risk vendors.

Contracts must include confidentiality obligations, breach notification timelines, data processing terms, and right-to-audit language when applicable.

## Evidence

Acceptable evidence includes vendor questionnaires, security reports, contract clauses, risk ratings, and renewal review records.
