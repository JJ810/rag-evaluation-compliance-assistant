# Incident Response Policy

## Purpose

This synthetic policy defines incident response expectations for security and privacy events.

## Requirements

Potential security incidents must be triaged within four business hours after detection or internal report.

Critical incidents must be escalated to the Chief Information Security Officer within one hour after severity classification.

The incident commander must maintain a timeline of decisions, affected systems, evidence collected, and containment actions.

Post-incident reviews must be completed within five business days after incident closure. Corrective actions must have accountable owners and due dates.

## Evidence

Acceptable evidence includes incident tickets, escalation records, forensic notes, post-incident review documents, and corrective action trackers.
