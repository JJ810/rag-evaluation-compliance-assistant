# Access Control Policy

## Purpose

This synthetic policy defines baseline access controls for enterprise systems used in the portfolio demo. It does not contain confidential or production information.

## Requirements

Employees must use multi-factor authentication for remote access, privileged access, and administrative consoles. Shared user accounts are not permitted for business applications.

Access must follow least-privilege principles. Managers approve new access requests, and application owners review privileged access at least once per quarter.

Joiner, mover, and leaver access changes must be completed within 24 hours after the approved HR or manager event is received by IT.

Service accounts must have a named owner, documented business purpose, and credentials rotated every 90 days or sooner if compromise is suspected.

## Evidence

Acceptable evidence includes access review records, ticket approvals, identity provider logs, and service account inventory exports.
